# Vantaa Participatory Budgeting (Osallistuva Budjetointi) — Citizen-Proposed & Implemented Projects

## Overview

Vantaa's participatory budgeting platform, **Osallistuva Vantaa** ([osallistuvavantaa.fi](https://osallistuvavantaa.fi/)), lets residents propose, develop, and vote on how municipal funds are spent. The platform runs on Maptionnaire and covers the entire city. Vantaa has run participatory budgeting in multiple rounds since 2020, scaling from two districts to city-wide.

**Total budget (2023–2025 citywide round):** EUR 1.2 million, divided by population across seven major regions.

### Implementation Status (as of end 2024)

Per the Vantaa 2024 financial statement:

- **Implementation plans** for all 66 projects were completed by August 31, 2024
- **Some projects were already implemented during 2024**, including:
  - Additional waste bins in the Myyrmäki area (continuing previous round)
  - Seasonal lights installed on Rälssitie
  - Seasonal light competition in Tikkurila (organized jointly)
  - **Lammaslampi pedestrian bridge renovation** — the single largest Streets & Parks OSBU expenditure in 2024 (EUR 50,000 of the EUR 50,000 spent on new OSBU projects in 2024)
- **Remaining projects** (approx. EUR 510,000 worth, mainly green areas and sports facilities) are scheduled for implementation during 2025
- Some projects may shift to later dates due to the large number of simultaneous implementations
- Project progress is reported three times per year to the city center development plan monitoring groups
