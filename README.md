Osallistuva Vantaa is the City of Vantaa’s digital civic participation platform. It combines
participatory budgeting, public consultation, idea collection, surveys, co-design
workshops, and resident voting into one system. Think of it as a “digital democracy
operating system” for the city.
What it actually is
At its core, the platform allows residents to:
propose ideas for improving the city
discuss and refine proposals
vote on projects
track implementation
participate in planning processes
answer surveys and consultations
The flagship use case is participatory budgeting (PB):
the city allocates a real budget (in Vantaa’s case €1.2 million in 2023–2025), and citizens
decide how that money is spent.
Examples of funded projects included:
outdoor gyms
youth recreation programs
movie nights
park improvements
neurodiversity training in schools
local events and recreation activities
So this is not just a feedback website.
It is a structured civic participation workflow tied to real municipal execution.
