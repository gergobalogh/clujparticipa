# JTBD Job Stories — social-sharing

## JOB-006: Civic Advocacy

**Job statement**: When I find an idea I believe in, I want to spread the word to my community, so I can rally support and increase its chances of being selected.

### Job Dimensions

**Functional**: Share a civic idea link with my personal network (WhatsApp, Facebook, direct link) with minimal friction — one tap, not copy-paste-compose.

**Emotional**: Feel like an effective advocate — my sharing matters, it reaches people, and it produces visible results (more votes, more awareness). Avoid the embarrassment of sharing a broken or blank link.

**Social**: Be seen as someone who contributes constructively to community improvement, not a complainer or spammer. When I share, I want the link to look credible and professional so my network takes it seriously.

---

## JOB-007: Credible First Impression

**Job statement**: When someone shares a civic idea link with me, I want to see at a glance what the idea is about and whether it's worth clicking, so I don't waste time on irrelevant content.

### Job Dimensions

**Functional**: See a rich preview (title, image, category, vote count) in my social feed or messaging app before deciding to click.

**Emotional**: Feel that the platform is legitimate and professional — a blank link feels like spam; a rich preview feels like a real civic initiative.

**Social**: If I click through because the preview was compelling, I'm more likely to share it further — good previews compound sharing behavior.
