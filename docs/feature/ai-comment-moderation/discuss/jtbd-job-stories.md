# JTBD Job Stories — ai-comment-moderation

## JOB-008: Auto-moderate toxic content

**Functional job**: When a resident posts a comment, I want the platform to screen it for toxicity and hate speech before it becomes visible, so I can trust that the discussion space is safe without waiting for a human moderator to notice.

**Emotional job**: When I see a comment section, I want to feel that someone is watching out for abuse, so I don't feel anxious about encountering harassment on a civic platform.

**Social job**: When I tell neighbors about the platform, I want to say "it keeps things civil automatically," so I'm recommending a safe space rather than an unmoderated free-for-all.

## JOB-009: Scale moderation beyond manual review

**Functional job**: When my pilot gets more traffic, I want to trust that every comment is screened consistently, so I don't have to read every single comment myself or worry that something slipped through overnight.

**Emotional job**: When I wake up and check the platform, I want to feel confident that no toxic content has been sitting publicly for hours, so I'm not anxious about what I'll find.

**Social job**: When I report to the coalition, I want to show that moderation is automated and consistent, so I'm seen as a responsible platform operator rather than someone who lets abuse fester.

## JOB-010: Preserve my voice when AI gets it wrong

**Functional job**: When my comment is flagged by the AI moderator, I want to understand why and have a path to contest it, so a false positive doesn't permanently silence my contribution.

**Emotional job**: When my comment is held for review, I want to feel respected and heard, not arbitrarily censored by a machine.

**Social job**: When I see that flagged comments get human review, I feel the platform values my participation even when the algorithm is uncertain.
