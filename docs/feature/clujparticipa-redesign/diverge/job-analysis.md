# JTBD Analysis — clujparticipa-redesign

## 1. Raw Request

Redesign the ClujParticipa civic participation platform — moving from a basic prototype to a polished, trustworthy civic UI that makes residents believe their engagement matters.

Source: `CONCEPT.md` + redesign branch work (editorial civic UI redesign, tasks 1-21).

## 2. Job Extraction — 5 Whys Chain

| Level | Question | Answer |
|-------|----------|--------|
| Tactical | What are users doing? | Submitting ideas, voting, commenting on a web platform |
| Operational | Why does this workflow exist? | Because there is no structured channel between residents and city decisions |
| Strategic | What decision is being pursued? | Residents need to decide whether engaging with the city is worth their time — they need proof that input leads to visible outcomes |
| Physical | What's the irreducible function? | **Input → Visible Tracking → Demonstrated Impact** — reduce the gap between citizen input and visible city response |

## 3. Job Statements

**Functional (required):**
When I see a problem in my neighborhood, I want to submit my idea and track whether it gets acted on, so I can decide whether engaging with the city is worth my time.

**Emotional:**
When I submit an idea for my neighborhood, I want to feel that my contribution matters and is taken seriously, so I don't regret investing my time and hope.

**Social:**
When I participate in civic improvements, I want to be seen as someone who cares about my community, so I can build credibility as an engaged resident rather than a complainer.

## 4. Disruption Check

Is there a higher-level job that makes this entire job unnecessary? Yes: "Ensure city government acts on resident priorities without requiring residents to chase outcomes." If the city proactively published decisions tied to resident input, the tracking job would dissolve. **However**, in the Romanian institutional context (low trust, politicization risk), this higher-level job cannot be fulfilled by technology alone — it requires institutional change. The platform serves as a forcing function: making the tracking visible creates pressure for institutional responsiveness.

**Conclusion**: The physical-level job (Input → Visible Tracking → Demonstrated Impact) is the right abstraction. Not tactical (better forms), not life-goal (perfect democracy).
