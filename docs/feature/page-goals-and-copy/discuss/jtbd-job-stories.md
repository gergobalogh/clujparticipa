# JTBD Job Stories — page-goals-and-copy

## Public Pages

### J-HOME: Homepage Scanner
**When** I land on clujparticipa.ro for the first time, **I want to** understand within 10 seconds what this platform does, that it's alive, and what I can do here, **so I can** decide whether to stay and explore or leave.

- Functional: Scan the page for purpose, activity signals, and entry points
- Emotional: Feel reassured that this is a real, active platform — not an abandoned prototype
- Social: Be able to tell a neighbor "I found this civic site, it looks legit"

### J-HOME-RETURN: Homepage Returner
**When** I come back after submitting an idea, **I want to** see evidence that things are moving (new ideas, status changes, completed count), **so I can** feel my participation matters and the platform isn't abandoned.

- Functional: Quickly assess platform health and recent activity
- Emotional: Feel validated that my earlier engagement mattered
- Social: See that others are participating too (social proof)

### J-IDEAS: Ideas Browser
**When** I want to find ideas relevant to my neighborhood or interests, **I want to** filter and sort the idea list efficiently, **so I can** discover what people in my district are proposing and decide whether to support something.

- Functional: Find relevant ideas by district, category, or status
- Emotional: Feel empowered by having control over what I see
- Social: See that my neighbors are active and engaged

### J-DETAIL: Idea Deep-Diver
**When** I find an idea that interests me, **I want to** understand the full picture — description, status pipeline, feasibility, comments, and progress — **so I can** decide whether to support it, comment on it, or share it.

- Functional: Get complete information to make a participation decision
- Emotional: Feel informed and confident in my support decision
- Social: See community engagement (comments, support count) as social proof

### J-SUBMIT: Idea Proposer
**When** I see a problem in my neighborhood, **I want to** submit my idea quickly with minimal friction, **so I can** contribute without it feeling like a bureaucratic process.

- Functional: Submit a complete idea (title, description, location, category) in under 5 minutes
- Emotional: Feel heard — my idea is acknowledged, not swallowed by a void
- Social: Be part of a growing list of community proposals

### J-MAP: Geographic Explorer
**When** I want to see where ideas are concentrated geographically, **I want to** explore the map with filters, **so I can** spot activity in my area or discover gaps where my neighborhood needs proposals.

- Functional: Visualize idea distribution across the city
- Emotional: Feel connection to place — ideas are anchored in real locations
- Social: See which neighborhoods are active and which are underserved

### J-HOW: Process Learner
**When** I'm unsure how the platform works or what happens after I submit an idea, **I want to** see a clear pipeline walkthrough, **so I can** set realistic expectations and trust the process.

- Functional: Understand the full idea lifecycle from submission to completion
- Emotional: Feel confident that there IS a process — this isn't a black hole
- Social: Be able to explain the process to others credibly

### J-RESULTS: Outcome Verifier
**When** I hear that voting happened or ideas were selected, **I want to** see the results transparently, **so I can** verify the process is fair and ideas are actually moving forward.

- Functional: See which ideas were selected, vote counts, voting periods
- Emotional: Feel the system delivers real outcomes, not just collects ideas
- Social: Share results as proof that participation works

### J-AUTH: Identity Connector
**When** I want to vote or comment and need to prove I'm a real person (not a bot), **I want to** sign in with an account I already have, **so I can** participate without creating yet another password.

- Functional: Authenticate with existing Google/Facebook account
- Emotional: Feel the platform respects my time — no new passwords
- Social: Feel that my authenticated participation carries more weight

## Utility/Legal Pages

### J-PRIVACY: Trust Builder
**When** I'm concerned about how my data is used, **I want to** read a clear, jargon-free privacy notice, **so I can** decide whether to engage honestly without feeling surveilled.

- Functional: Understand what data is collected, how it's used, how to delete it
- Emotional: Feel respected and informed, not tricked
- Social: Be able to recommend the platform as privacy-respecting

### J-DATA-DELETE: Data Remover
**When** I want to remove my personal data from the platform, **I want to** find a clear deletion path, **so I can** exercise my GDPR rights without contacting support.

- Functional: Request and confirm data deletion
- Emotional: Feel in control of my personal information
- Social: Know the platform honors legal obligations

### J-TERMS: Legal Clarifier
**When** I want to understand my rights and obligations on the platform, **I want to** read concise terms, **so I can** know what's binding and what's prototype-only.

- Functional: Understand non-binding status, content rules, liability limits
- Emotional: Feel the platform is transparent about its prototype nature
- Social: Know the legal framework before participating publicly

### J-BUG: Problem Reporter
**When** I encounter a bug or broken feature, **I want to** report it easily, **so I can** help improve the platform without going through complex support channels.

- Functional: Submit a bug description with optional URL and email
- Emotional: Feel that my report matters and will be read
- Social: Contribute as a helpful user, not just a complainer

## Internal Pages

### J-ADMIN: Platform Operator
**When** I need to manage ideas, moderate comments, or update statuses, **I want to** use a focused admin interface, **so I can** keep the platform running and respond to community needs promptly.

- Functional: Change idea statuses, review flagged comments, post feasibility notes, manage voting
- Emotional: Feel in control of the platform's health and community trust
- Social: Be seen as a responsible steward by coalition partners
