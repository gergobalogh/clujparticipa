# Outcome KPIs — page-goals-and-copy

| KPI | Target | Measurement method | Baseline |
|-----|--------|--------------------|----------|
| Homepage bounce rate | Decrease by 15% | Umami analytics: % of homepage visitors who leave without clicking any link | Unknown (no baseline yet — measure for 2 weeks pre-launch) |
| Idea submission completion rate | Increase by 20% | Umami analytics: % of visitors who start the submission form and complete it | Unknown — measure form start vs. form submit events |
| "How It Works" → submission conversion | ≥10% of How It Works visitors click "Submit your idea" | Umami analytics: click CTA on /how-it-works | Unknown |
| Auth page → OAuth click-through rate | Increase by 10% | Umami analytics: % of login page visitors who click an OAuth button | Unknown |
| Results page engagement | ≥30% of visitors click a selected idea | Umami analytics: click-through on /results | Unknown |
| Post-copy homepage time-on-page | Increase by 20% | Umami analytics: average session duration on / | Unknown |

**Note**: These KPIs depend on Umami analytics being operational (feature `umami-analytics`). If analytics are not yet deployed, KPIs are tracked manually for 2 weeks post-launch using page-specific event counts.
