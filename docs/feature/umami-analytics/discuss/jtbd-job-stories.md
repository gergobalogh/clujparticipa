# JTBD Job Stories — umami-analytics

## JOB-002: Operator Diagnoses Drop-off

**Statement**: When I see few ideas being submitted or users leaving without acting, I want to see exactly where in the journey they drop off and what they did before leaving, so I can fix the broken step before more users lose trust in the platform.

**Functional dimension**: Identify which page, button, or form field causes abandonment; measure conversion from landing → submit → vote → return.

**Emotional dimension**: When I see low engagement numbers, I want to feel confident I know WHY rather than guessing, so I can act decisively instead of hoping.

**Social dimension**: When I report progress to the coalition, I want to show data-backed evidence that the platform is improving, so I am seen as a credible steward of civic resources.

---

## JOB-003: Operator Validates Feature Value

**Statement**: When I invest effort in a new feature (editorial redesign, pipeline track, notification), I want to measure whether it actually changes user behavior, so I can double down on what works and cut what doesn't.

**Functional dimension**: Compare before/after metrics for feature launches; see which features drive engagement vs. which are unused.

**Emotional dimension**: When I ship a feature, I want to feel certainty that it mattered, rather than shipping into a void and never knowing.

**Social dimension**: When I discuss priorities with the team, I want to bring evidence of what users actually do, so I'm not just advocating from opinion.

---

## JOB-004: Resident Trusts Platform Privacy

**Statement**: When I visit a civic participation site, I want to know that my behavior on the site isn't being sold or profiled, so I can engage honestly without feeling surveilled.

**Functional dimension**: See a clear, honest privacy notice about what is tracked and why; ability to opt out without losing core functionality.

**Emotional dimension**: When I see a tracking notice, I want to feel respected and informed, not tricked or watched.

**Social dimension**: When I tell neighbors about this platform, I want to say "it respects your privacy" with confidence, so I'm not recommending a surveillance tool.

---

## JOB-005: Resident Sees Platform Is Alive

**Statement**: When I return to the platform after weeks away, I want to see evidence that things are happening (new ideas, status changes, votes), so I feel my participation matters and the platform isn't abandoned.

**Functional dimension**: See fresh activity indicators — recent ideas, moving statuses, vote counts — that prove the platform is active.

**Emotional dimension**: When I see stale content, I feel like I wasted my time. When I see movement, I feel hopeful.

**Social dimension**: When I see others participating actively, I feel part of a community effort rather than shouting into the void.
